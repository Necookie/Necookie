# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors to the Necookie GitHub profile, especially engineers, collaborators, and curious peers, arrive to understand the person behind the repositories. They are scanning quickly, often inside GitHub’s familiar interface, for a credible sense of technical interests and working style.

## Product Purpose

This profile README is a compact personal systems notebook for Dheyn Michael Orlanda, known as Necookie. It introduces an AI Engineer and Software Developer in the Philippines, documents a real research direction, and gives GitHub’s pinned work room to speak for itself. Success is quiet clarity: a visitor should leave with a durable impression of careful, practical engineering.

## Brand Personality

Methodical, curious, and understated. The voice is factual rather than promotional, technical without cosplay, and personal without becoming a social landing page. It should feel like a well-kept engineering field note, not a job application or an AI-generated portfolio.

## Anti-references

Avoid profile-README template stacks, giant logo walls, scrolling widgets, contribution snakes, stats badges, quote widgets, watermarked typing GIFs, and social/contact call-to-actions. Avoid generic AI imagery, neural brains, glossy cards, gradients, fan art, inflated claims, and job-seeking language. Do not render existing Ghibli, soot, or sakura imagery.

## Design Principles

1. Make the research direction concrete through an honest conceptual map, not speculative implementation claims.
2. Use structure as personality: labels, grids, margins, and technical notation should do the expressive work.
3. Keep the profile left-aligned and skimmable so GitHub remains the primary interface.
4. Let a small original cookie-cat seal add recognition without becoming a mascot brand.
5. Prefer a short, specific working set over exhaustive technology inventory.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high-contrast light and dark graphics, semantic Markdown headings, meaningful image alt text, and SVG text large enough to remain legible on a narrow screen. Do not rely on color alone to communicate the research flow. Keep motion out of the rendered profile.
