# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors to the Necookie GitHub profile, especially engineers, collaborators, and curious peers, arrive to understand the person behind the repositories. They are scanning quickly, often inside GitHub’s familiar interface, for a credible sense of technical interests and working style.

## Product Purpose

This profile README is a compact personal introduction for Dheyn Michael Orlanda, known as Necookie. It introduces an AI Engineer and Software Developer in the Philippines, documents a real research direction, and gives GitHub’s pinned work room to speak for itself. Success is quiet clarity: a visitor should leave with a durable impression of careful, practical engineering.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, and lightly playful. The voice is factual rather than promotional, technical without cosplay, and personal without becoming a social landing page. The artwork should feel like a quiet late-night workspace, not a job application or a generic AI portfolio.

## Anti-references

Avoid profile-README template stacks, giant logo walls, scrolling widgets, contribution snakes, stats badges, quote widgets, watermarked GIFs, and social/contact call-to-actions. Avoid cyberpunk AI clichés, neural brains, holograms, fake interface text, glossy cards, inflated claims, and job-seeking language.

## Design Principles

1. Use one compact anime illustration as atmosphere, not as the entire identity.
2. Keep motion ambient and quiet so the GIF never competes with the writing.
3. Keep generated text out of the artwork; all factual language belongs in Markdown.
4. Keep the profile short and skimmable so GitHub remains the primary interface.
5. Prefer a specific working set over an exhaustive technology inventory.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic Markdown headings, meaningful image alt text, a compact responsive image width, and a static PNG fallback when reduced motion is requested. Motion must not carry information.
